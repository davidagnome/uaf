#   relative-path|format|width|height|sha256|meanRGB|topLeftQuadrantMeanRGB

# BMP and PCX are lossless and simple enough that any two decoders agree byte for byte.

# SDL3_image was built against may differ by a unit or two per channel. JPEG is therefore
# checked against the two means with a tolerance instead of by hash. The quadrant mean is
# there because a whole-image mean survives a sheared or vertically flipped decode, which is
# exactly the failure a pitch or row-order mistake produces.
